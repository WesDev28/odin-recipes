Spaghetti & Meatballs

Description

This simple recipe brings a lot of bang for your buck.

Ingridients List

  • 6 beef meatballs
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 can of chopped tomatoes
  • 1 serving of spaghetti
  • 1 tablespoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Dice onion.
  3. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat; cook and stir onions in hot oil until lightly sauteed, about 3 minutes.
  4. Add meatballs to the skillet. Leave cooking until piping hot throughout; no pink.
  5. Bring a pot of water to a rolling boil.
  6. Add spaghetti into boiling water with a pinch of salt and a dash of olive oil
  7. Wait until spaghetti is brough back to a boil - use fork to find al dente feel of pasta - roughly 6 minutes.
  8. Add your can of tomatoes to the skillet with onions & meatballs. Stir for 5 minutes until sauce thickens up.
  9. Drain pasta; lightly season with salt, black pepper, and a dash of olive oil. Then stir.
  10. Either mix sauce and pasta or serve sauce on top of pasta.
  11. And enjoy!
