Beef Tacos

Click to see all recipes

Description

Enjoy Tacos served with cheese - but not too many.

Ingridients List

  • 250 grams lean ground beef
  • 1 medium sized onion
  • 200 grams cheddar cheese
  • 1 can of diced tomatoes
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 taco seasoning: Chilli powder, paprika, oregano, chilli flakes, salt, black pepper
  • 8 taco shells
  • Lettuce & any other toppings of choice

Steps

  1. Add diced onion to a large skillet at medium-to-high heat.
  2. Add ground beef to skillet, crumbling with wooden spoon, until brown / cooked through. Cook for 8-10 minutes.
  3. Add seasoning and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add cheese and tomatoes amd stir over a medium heat.
  5. Cook stiring constatly, until cheese is melted and mixture comes to a simmer.
  6. Cook for 3 more minutes and remove from heat.
  7. Add filling to taco shells and top with shredded lettuce and any other toppings of choice.
  8. Serve and enjoy!
